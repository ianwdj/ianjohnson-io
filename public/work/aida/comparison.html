What the CRM fields leave out

A customer’s email tells you what they said. The deal record tells you what happened: whether they signed, what they bought, and why the opportunity was lost.

Aida connects those details with calls and conversations so your team can answer questions that span the full relationship. In the examples below, that meant explaining cancellations, recognizing competitive deals, and tracing customer complaints across weeks.
